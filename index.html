Smart recommendations — plain language insights from your data
Coffee + Pastry is your most popular combo
3 out of 10 transactions included both Coffee and Pastry together. Customers who buy one almost always buy the other (60% confidence, lift 1.7×).
Create a "Morning Duo" bundle at a small discount to boost average spend.
Sandwich + Drink is a strong lunch pair
Every customer who bought a Sandwich also bought a Drink (100% confidence). This happened in 3 separate transactions across different customers.
Bundle Sandwich + Drink as a "Lunch Deal" — you'll almost certainly sell both together anyway.
Saturday is your biggest revenue day
Saturday (Jan 4) generated £16.50 — 26% of your weekly revenue. The morning slot (08–10h) sees the highest footfall across all days.
Make sure you're fully staffed on Saturday mornings. Consider a weekend-only special to drive even more traffic.
Tuesday is your slowest day — only £7.50 revenue
Tuesday (Jan 7) had just 2 transactions, both single-item purchases. Revenue was 55% lower than your best day.
Run a Tuesday promotion — "2-for-1 Pastry with any hot drink" — to convert single-item visits into bigger baskets.
Customer C001 is your most loyal — reward them
C001 visited 3 times in 5 days and spent £13.50, making them your top customer by both frequency and spend. They buy Coffee, Sandwich and Cake.
A simple loyalty card could retain C001 and encourage other occasional customers to visit more often.
4 customers visited only once — they may not return
C003, C004, C005, C006 each made a single visit. Without a reason to come back, one-time visitors are unlikely to return on their own.
Add a "Come back" card to every receipt — a small reward for the next visit converts one-timers into regulars.
